What GeneGuard does and doesn’t do

It teaches how genes, variants and inheritance work. It does not diagnose, predict your medical future, or replace a genetic counsellor.

Sequence lab

Paste a DNA sequence or FASTA, or load a file. Basic sequence statistics do not identify disease.

Gene explorer

A small curated set of well-documented genes. For the full catalogue use NCBI Gene.

Variant lookup

Check an identifier’s format, learn the terms, then open the public record. Live ClinVar and Ensembl retrieval needs a backend service, which this build does not include.

Terms

rsIDA dbSNP reference number for a known variant position, such as rs334.
Reference / alternate alleleReference is the base in the reference genome; alternate is the different base seen in some people.
HGVSA standard naming style, e.g. c. for coding DNA, p. for protein.
ClassificationClinVar labels range from benign to pathogenic, plus “uncertain significance”. Labels can change as evidence grows.

Disease associations

Three different ideas that are easy to confuse.

Association

A statistical link between a gene or variant and a condition in studies.

Predisposition

A higher or lower chance of developing a condition. Most people with a risk variant never develop it.

Diagnosis

Made by a clinician from symptoms, tests and history. Never by this app.

Family history

Map relatives and any conditions they reported. GeneGuard draws the tree and calculates no inheritance probabilities.

Assistant

An offline educational assistant. It answers from a built-in glossary and the gene set above, and says so when it doesn’t know.
